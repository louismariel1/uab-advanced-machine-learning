Your situation may be stronger than simply being a dispute over a ticket refund.

The Institut national de la consommation (INC) specifically discusses Royal Air Maroc in connection with EU Regulation 261/2004. It notes that the EU rules can apply where a journey starts at an EU airport, including certain connecting itineraries involving a non-EU carrier.

There is also a particularly important procedural development in France: since 7 February 2026, for certain aviation claims under Regulation 261/2004, a consumer must generally first use consumer mediation before bringing a court action. The INC also notes that the limitation period for bringing the claim remains five years from the event.
